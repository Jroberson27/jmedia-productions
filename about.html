About Jordan · The Throughline

It Was Always
There

For over fifteen years it has been the same three things. Performance, storytelling, and discipline. It started on a drumline and competitive stages where timing and precision were everything, and it has carried into every room since.

02 · On set

Production run like a professional set

Jordan has worked camera department roles, including Camera Utility and 1st AC, on Universal Orlando campaigns, plus production roles on commercials for brands like Heinz, Carvana and Foot Locker. Businesses get shoots planned and run with that same discipline, so the crew moves quickly and your team's time is respected.

03 · Business development

Creative that starts with the commercial goal

Jordan spent a decade in business development and enterprise sales, including a founding sales role at a hospitality technology company. That work taught him how decision makers think and what a story has to do to move them, which is where every JMEDIA brief begins.

04 · Running JMEDIA

A founder who creates for his own audience too

Jordan runs JMEDIA Productions and cohosts Meet The Robersons, a family theme park channel, including content made through Universal Orlando's Fanny Pack creator program. Creating for his own audience keeps him close to how people actually watch today.
