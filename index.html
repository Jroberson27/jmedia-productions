How a project runs

  1. 01

    Step 1: Discovery

    We learn the business, the audience, what's working today and what the content needs to do.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Creative planning

    Concepts, scripts or shot lists, locations, people on camera and a schedule, all agreed before the shoot. Some projects feature the owner, others feature the team, customers or the product. The story decides.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Production

    A professionally run shoot, planned so your team's time is used well.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Postproduction

    Editing, color, sound, graphics and captions, with time built in for your feedback.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Delivery

    Final files formatted for every place they will run, from your website to each social platform.

  6. 06 Ongoing content support

    Step 6: Publishing and optimization

    We schedule and publish the content, review how it performs and use what we learn to shape the next round.

Steps one through five are part of every project. Step six is for businesses that want ongoing content support.
